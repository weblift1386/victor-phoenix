Service Policy

This policy explains how Victor Phoenix handles digital service requests, projects, communication, revisions, delivery, and client responsibilities.

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Service Policy Refund Policy Cookie Policy Disclaimer

1. Introduction

Victor Phoenix provides digital and creative services for individuals, businesses, brands, and organizations.

This Service Policy explains the general process used when a client requests and receives a service from Victor Phoenix.

Specific projects may have additional requirements or terms depending on the service and package selected.

2. Services We Provide

Our services may include:

  • Logo and brand identity design
  • Flyer and promotional graphics
  • Banner and social media graphics
  • Website design
  • Website development
  • Web application development
  • App development
  • Digital marketing services
  • AI-assisted digital solutions
  • Other digital and creative services

Services and packages may be added, changed, updated, or discontinued as Victor Phoenix evolves.

3. Choosing a Service

Clients should carefully review the available service or package before placing an order.

Service information may include:

  • Service name
  • Package level
  • Included features
  • Price
  • Estimated delivery time
  • Revision allowance
  • Required client materials

If you are unsure which service or package is appropriate, you may contact Victor Phoenix before placing an order.

4. Service Process

A typical project may follow these steps:

  • Client selects a service or package.
  • Client provides the required project information.
  • Project requirements are reviewed and confirmed.
  • Payment arrangements are confirmed.
  • Work begins after the project is approved to start.
  • Initial work or design is produced.
  • Applicable revisions are completed.
  • Final work is delivered.

The exact process may vary depending on the type and complexity of the project.

5. Client Materials

Clients may need to provide materials required for their project.

These may include:

  • Business information
  • Brand names and descriptions
  • Images
  • Logos
  • Written content
  • Design preferences
  • Website information
  • Other relevant project materials

Clients are responsible for ensuring that materials they provide are accurate and that they have the necessary rights to use them.

6. Delivery Time

Delivery times are normally stated on the relevant service or package page.

Some simple projects may have a delivery target of approximately 48 hours or 2 days, where specifically stated.

Delivery estimates may change when a project requires additional information, revisions, technical work, or client approvals.

The delivery period may also be affected by delays outside Victor Phoenix's reasonable control.

7. Communication

Project communication may take place through the official contact channels provided by Victor Phoenix.

Clients should provide clear instructions and respond to project questions or requests for information in a timely manner.

Delayed communication may affect the project's delivery schedule.

8. Revisions

Revision availability depends on the service or package purchased.

A revision means a reasonable adjustment to work already created within the agreed project requirements.

A request that substantially changes the original concept, direction, scope, or requirements may be treated as additional work.

9. Changes to Project Scope

Once a project has started, clients may request additional features, pages, designs, concepts, functionality, or other work.

Work outside the original agreement may require:

  • Additional payment
  • Additional delivery time
  • A revised project agreement

10. Client Approval

Clients are responsible for reviewing submitted work and communicating any applicable corrections or revisions within the agreed revision period.

Once final work has been approved, further changes may be treated as a new request depending on the circumstances.

11. Website and App Projects

Website and application projects may require additional information and technical decisions compared with standard graphic design services.

Depending on the project, clients may need to provide or approve:

  • Website content
  • Brand assets
  • Domain information
  • Hosting information
  • Third-party service access
  • Application requirements
  • Functional requirements

Third-party hosting, domain, software, API, payment, or other external costs may not be included unless explicitly stated in the selected package.

12. Third-Party Tools and Platforms

Some projects may use third-party platforms, hosting providers, APIs, AI tools, payment processors, design tools, or other external technologies.

The availability and functionality of these services may be outside Victor Phoenix's control.

13. Project Completion

A project is considered completed when the agreed work has been delivered according to the selected service or package and applicable revisions have been addressed.

Additional requests after completion may be treated as a new project or additional service.

14. Client Responsibility

Clients are responsible for checking final materials before using them publicly or commercially.

This includes checking:

  • Names
  • Dates
  • Prices
  • Contact information
  • Spelling
  • Business information
  • Other supplied content

15. Quality and Service Standards

Victor Phoenix aims to provide professional, functional, and carefully prepared digital work according to the agreed requirements.

However, creative preferences can differ between clients. Clients are encouraged to provide clear references, requirements, and feedback before and during the project.

16. Refunds

Refund requests are handled according to the Victor Phoenix Refund Policy.

Please review the Refund Policy before placing an order.

17. Client Information and Privacy

Information provided by clients in connection with a project may be used to communicate, process, manage, and deliver the requested service.

For more information, please review the Privacy Policy.

18. Policy Updates

Victor Phoenix may update this Service Policy when necessary to reflect changes in services, packages, processes, or business operations.

The latest version published on this website will apply to future service requests unless otherwise agreed.

19. Contact

If you have questions about a service, package, delivery time, revision, project requirement, or any other service-related matter, please contact Victor Phoenix through the official contact channel provided on the website.

20. Effective Date

This Service Policy is effective from the date it is published on the Victor Phoenix website and may be updated from time to time.
