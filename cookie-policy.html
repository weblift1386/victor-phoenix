Cookie Policy

1. Introduction

Victor Phoenix may use cookies and similar technologies to help operate, secure, analyze, and improve its website.

This policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used, and the choices available to visitors.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a visitor's device when the website is accessed.

Cookies can help websites remember information, understand how visitors use the website, maintain functionality, and improve the user experience.

3. Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for certain website functions to operate properly.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics services are used, cookies may help us understand how visitors interact with the website, such as which pages are visited and how the website is used.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may help remember certain preferences or settings where such functionality is available.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into the website may place their own cookies or similar technologies.

4. Why We May Use Cookies

Cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Keeping the website functioning properly
  • Improving website performance
  • Understanding website usage
  • Remembering certain preferences
  • Improving user experience
  • Supporting security and fraud prevention

5. Third-Party Services

Victor Phoenix may use third-party technologies or services that place cookies or collect technical information.

Examples may include website hosting, analytics, security, embedded content, payment services, or other technologies used to operate the website.

The policies of the relevant third-party providers may also apply to their use of cookies and similar technologies.

6. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through their browser settings.

You may be able to configure your browser to block cookies, notify you when cookies are being used, or delete stored cookies.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some parts of a website.

7. Similar Technologies

In some circumstances, technologies other than traditional cookies may be used to perform similar functions, such as storing information locally on a device or collecting technical information about website usage.

8. Cookies and Personal Information

Some cookie-related information may be associated with other information collected through the website.

For more information about how Victor Phoenix handles personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

Victor Phoenix may update this Cookie Policy when website technologies, services, or business practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact
