Refund Policy

This policy explains how refund requests are handled for services purchased from Victor Phoenix.

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Service Policy Refund Policy Cookie Policy Disclaimer

1. Introduction

Victor Phoenix aims to provide professional digital services according to the requirements agreed with each client.

Because many of our services involve custom work created specifically for a client, refund eligibility may depend on the stage of the project and the circumstances surrounding the request.

2. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a client requests cancellation before work has started, Victor Phoenix may consider a refund depending on whether any work, preparation, planning, resource allocation, or third-party costs have already been incurred.

Any applicable refund will be determined based on the circumstances of the specific project.

3. Cancellation After Work Begins

Once work has started, a full refund may not be available because time, resources, creative work, development work, or other project activities may already have been completed.

Where appropriate, Victor Phoenix may review the project and determine whether a partial refund or alternative resolution is reasonable.

4. Completed or Delivered Work

Refunds are generally not available for work that has already been completed and delivered according to the agreed service requirements.

Clients should review delivered work and communicate any applicable revision requests within the revision period associated with their service or package.

5. Change of Mind

A change of mind after work has started does not automatically qualify for a refund.

Victor Phoenix may consider the circumstances of the request, the stage of the project, and the amount of work already completed.

6. Client Delays

Refunds may not be available where a project is delayed because the client does not provide required information, materials, approvals, or feedback within a reasonable timeframe.

Where possible, Victor Phoenix will communicate with the client to determine how the project can continue.

7. Incorrect Client Information

Clients are responsible for checking the accuracy of the information they provide.

If incorrect information supplied by a client results in additional work or revisions, the client may be responsible for the additional work where applicable.

8. Third-Party Services

If a project depends on a third-party service, platform, hosting provider, software, API, payment provider, or other external service, refunds for that third-party service may be subject to that provider's own policies.

Victor Phoenix cannot guarantee refunds for fees charged directly by third-party providers.

9. Technical Problems

If a delivered digital service experiences a technical issue that falls within the agreed scope of the project, Victor Phoenix may first attempt to investigate and correct the issue where reasonably possible.

A technical issue does not automatically result in a refund.

10. Creative Preferences

Differences in personal taste or creative preference do not automatically qualify for a refund.

Clients should use the revision process included with their selected package to request reasonable adjustments within the agreed project scope.

11. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you qualify for a refund, contact Victor Phoenix through the official contact channel provided on the website.

Your request should include:

  • Your name or business name
  • The service or package purchased
  • The date of the order
  • The reason for the refund request
  • Any relevant project information

12. Refund Review

Each refund request will be reviewed based on the individual circumstances of the project.

Victor Phoenix may consider factors including:

  • Whether work has started
  • How much work has been completed
  • Whether the work has been delivered
  • Whether revisions remain available
  • Whether third-party costs were incurred
  • The reason for cancellation
  • The original service agreement

13. Alternative Resolutions

Where a refund is not appropriate, Victor Phoenix may, at its discretion, offer another reasonable solution depending on the circumstances.

This may include additional clarification, applicable revisions, correction of an agreed technical issue, or another mutually acceptable resolution.

14. No Guaranteed Business Results

Payment for a Victor Phoenix service is payment for the agreed digital service itself and does not guarantee a specific amount of revenue, sales, customers, followers, downloads, rankings, or other business results.

15. Changes to This Policy

Victor Phoenix may update this Refund Policy when necessary to reflect changes in services, payment processes, business practices, or applicable requirements.

The latest version published on this website will apply to future orders unless otherwise agreed.

16. Related Policies

This Refund Policy should be read together with the other Victor Phoenix policies:

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms & Conditions
  • Service Policy
  • Cookie Policy
  • Disclaimer

17. Contact

For questions regarding a refund or payment, please contact Victor Phoenix through the official contact method provided on the website.

18. Effective Date

This Refund Policy is effective from the date it is published on the Victor Phoenix website and may be updated from time to time.
